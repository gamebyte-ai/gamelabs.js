DevUtils

Built-in development tools accessible via this.devUtils in your app class or diContainer.getInstance(IDevUtils):

  • devUtils.logger — on-screen log panel. logger.log(msg, type?), logger.show(bool)
  • devUtils.statsPanel — FPS/render stats. statsPanel.show(bool)
  • devUtils.groundGrid — 3D ground grid helper. groundGrid.show(bool)
